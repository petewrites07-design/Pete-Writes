✍️ About Pete & Website

About Pete

I am an independent random fiction writer and a BSIT graduate. I started writing on Wattpad back in 2017 and created my official Facebook writing page in 2021. I write stories simply because it brings me joy, and I want people to explore my thoughts and imagination. Above all, I hope my stories can impart meaningful life lessons that can help and inspire readers in their own journeys.

About the Website

This website was created exclusively for my readers as a free reading platform. Readers can easily navigate through different categories to explore and enjoy my works. The primary goal of this website is simple and pure: to provide a comfortable, accessible, and free space for everyone to read and enjoy my stories. Maaari mong basahin ang lahat ng mga kwentong ito nang libre! Enjoy reading, relax, and immerse yourself in every chapter.
